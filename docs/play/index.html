Keyboard

  • / search · N new patient · T today
  • Alt+←/→ previous / next month
  • Enter in a row saves and moves to the next row; on the last row it adds one
  • Ctrl+Enter adds a row · Esc closes a panel
  • Ctrl+P prints the register · D dark mode
  • 1–5 jump between sections

Privacy & licence

  • Nothing leaves this device. No account, no analytics, no network request.
  • Data lives in this browser's localStorage. Clearing site data erases it — keep a backup.
  • PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. Free to use, study and share for noncommercial purposes.
  • Built by Dr. Sohil Momin. Source, releases and the full manual are on GitHub.

How the counts work

  • A row counts as a patient when any of OPD no., department, name, age, address, diagnosis or treatment is filled.
  • Gynecology is female-only — any department containing gyn, obs or obstet counts as female whatever the sex field says.
  • Under 15 counts as Pediatrics — a child entered under Medicine is also counted in the Pediatrics breakdown. Age accepts 5, 5y, 6 mo, 3 days, 1.5.
  • Day status (working, holiday, non-working, no activity) never changes a patient count — it labels the day for reporting.
